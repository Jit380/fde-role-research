# Role distinctions

**Compare responsibilities, not just titles.** These are practical interpretations of the sampled FDE descriptions, not universal definitions of neighboring jobs.

| Compared with… | What to inspect in an FDE posting |
| --- | --- |
| Product software engineer | How much time is spent discovering customer needs and working in their environment? Palantir adds direct stakeholder ownership to software delivery. |
| Solutions / sales engineer | Does ownership stop at a demo, or continue through production? Firecrawl spans demo, proof of concept, and deployment and also measures adoption/expansion. |
| Implementation consultant | Is the deliverable configuration, analysis, or production code? Middesk explicitly requires building and operating software systems. |
| Support engineer | Is work mainly reactive, or does it include design and end-to-end rollout? Firecrawl explicitly emphasizes adoption rather than ticket closure. |
| ML researcher | Is the task new model research or making existing AI work in an operational workflow? Pace lists integrations, evaluations, and agent troubleshooting. |

## Internship vs. new-grad vs. experienced

- **Internship:** limited-duration work and student eligibility where specified; real customer delivery can still be expected. Revel includes experienced-engineer collaboration; Palantir emphasizes immediate ownership.
- **New-grad:** a full-time entry role, with a specific graduation window in the Palantir example.
- **Experienced:** demonstrated production or customer-delivery history. Middesk and Firecrawl both state 3+ years, but in different kinds of experience.

## Questions worth asking in an interview

*Editorial checklist, based on the differences above.*

1. What do I own after the proof of concept reaches production?
2. How much time goes to coding, discovery, travel, and account work?
3. Who maintains customer-specific code, and what becomes core product?
4. What does success mean: reliability, customer outcomes, adoption, revenue, or all four?
5. What mentorship and scope limits apply to interns or new graduates?
