## Palantir — new-grad commercial {#palantir-new-grad-commercial}

**Forward Deployed Software Engineer, New Grad – Commercial** · [Primary posting](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/e500bcf3-19d8-4d3c-b340-4d76e4a55b40)

- **Work:** own customer projects from discovery to production; design systems with engineers; work with large data; build custom applications and LLM workflows; manage user and executive relationships.
- **Required:** engineering degree and proficiency in one or more languages, with Python, Java, C++, and TypeScript/JavaScript listed as examples.
- **Valued:** agency, ambiguity tolerance, and outcome ownership across technical and nontechnical teams.
- **Eligibility / logistics:** graduation in December 2026 or spring 2027; expected travel 25–50%, depending on team and location.
- **Published pay:** estimated $135,000–$145,000/year salary; potential stock and other incentives are additional.

## Middesk — production integrations {#middesk-production-integrations}

**Forward Deployed Engineer** · [Primary posting](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/middesk/a72b1048-1001-49b1-92bd-9c44574f939b)

- **Work:** build and operate API integrations, data pipelines, and AI workflows; own rollout and iteration; build evaluations and test harnesses; turn customer learning into reusable product improvements.
- **Required / sought:** 3+ years shipping production software; Python or Ruby plus another language; APIs, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, debugging, architecture judgment, customer communication, and LLM/agent experience including evaluations.
- **Preferred:** founder/startup background; fintech, identity, risk or compliance; developer tools, automation, reliability or observability.
- **Logistics:** hybrid in San Francisco or New York. Travel and sponsorship details not stated in the reviewed description.
- **Published pay:** $148,000–$255,000 compensation range, with equity indicated; verify package composition.

## Firecrawl — adoption and expansion {#firecrawl-adoption-and-expansion}

**Forward Deployed Engineer** · [Primary posting](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/firecrawl/bda40f47-a69b-44d4-ac1a-3f86f20d802d/)

- **Work:** diagnose extraction and pipeline problems; build proofs of concept and integrations; take customers to production; support adoption and expansion; relay product gaps.
- **Required / sought:** 3+ years customer-facing technical work or equivalent; ability to read code, use APIs, build integrations, and explain technical value to business audiences.
- **Preferred:** developer-tool, API, or AI-infrastructure experience. No named programming language is required in the reviewed text.
- **Logistics:** five days/week at SF headquarters. Posting names supported work-authorization pathways and excludes new H-1B sponsorship for candidates outside the US; read the source for details.
- **Published pay:** $209,000–$265,000/year **OTE** (on-target earnings), with commission and equity indicated. This is not a base-salary range.
