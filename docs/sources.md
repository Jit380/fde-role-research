## Selection and interpretation

- **Scope:** US-located roles across enterprise software, insurance AI, hardware, business verification, and developer infrastructure. Three internships, one new-grad role, two experienced roles; five employers.
- **Inclusion:** direct employer or employer-hosted applicant-tracking pages with readable duties and qualifications. Search results were discovery aids; the browser-rendered posting supplied the final facts.
- **Exclusion:** recruiter reposts, aggregators, duplicate location variants, and unverified snippets. This is a purposive comparison sample, not all current openings.
- **Normalization:** distinguish duties from requested qualifications and preferred experience. Preserve explicit dates and pay units. “Not stated” means absent from the reviewed text, not that the employer has no restriction.
- **Synthesis:** recurring themes are editorial interpretations linked to evidence. No frequency-based market claims; the Palantir pair repeats wording.

## Freshness and limitations

Job pages can change or disappear. Recheck dates, location, compensation, travel, and authorization details at the source before applying. Pace states May/June starts without a year; no year has been inferred. Revel's deadline is shown in Eastern time. Compensation figures describe different units and package types, so they are not ranked.

These notes summarize public descriptions rather than reproducing them. They do not assess personal eligibility or predict hiring outcomes.

## Update procedure

Open each source, confirm the title and application link, re-read qualifications and dates, then update the relevant role summary and this checked date. Mark closed or unavailable postings explicitly rather than silently presenting them as current.
