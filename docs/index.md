# What does an FDE actually do?

**A forward-deployed engineer turns a customer's messy operational problem into software that works in their environment.** The job combines engineering, problem discovery, deployment, and direct customer ownership.

This is a synthesis of **six live primary postings across five employers**, checked **November 12, 2026**. It is a US-focused research sample, not a ranking or an exhaustive job board. [Sources & limits →](./sources)

## The recurring work

1. **Discover:** talk to users, understand their workflow, and define a useful outcome.
2. **Build:** write applications, integrations, data pipelines, or specialized tools.
3. **Deploy:** make the solution reliable in the customer's real environment.
4. **Iterate:** debug failures, measure results, and feed reusable lessons into the product.

These stages recur in [Palantir](./full-time#palantir-new-grad-commercial), [Pace](./internships#pace-ai-workflows), and [Middesk](./full-time#middesk-production-integrations). Individual roles emphasize different stages.

## Three things to know first

- **Internships still involve real delivery.** Pace asks for projects and software foundations; prior AI experience is helpful, not required. [Pace posting](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/withpace/24c69367-3dfe-428e-98e6-0a6bf94a00e1)
- **FDE is not one technical stack.** Revel emphasizes hardware and systems; Middesk emphasizes APIs, cloud systems, and AI evaluations. [Revel](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/revel/34f97b81-c981-4c4b-9f4d-d471c33b46cc) · [Middesk](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/middesk/a72b1048-1001-49b1-92bd-9c44574f939b)
- **Read the constraints before the skills.** Graduation dates, travel, office attendance, and authorization language can determine fit. All six pages showed an application link when checked; availability can change.
